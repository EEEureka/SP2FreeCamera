## Native first-person target lock

- **Middle-click** terrain, a moving ground target or another aircraft's part to keep the native first-person camera looking at the selected point. Local cockpit/avatar colliders are excluded from this pick; a locked mouse cursor uses the viewport center.
- **Main-keyboard `-`** locks the game target selected at that moment. The camera follows that target's current position without changing the game's weapon target or automatically adopting later selection changes.
- Supports classic cockpit, player first-person (including seated FPV) and ordinary first-person camera parts. **Weapon/targeting pods, Orbit, Chase, Fly-by and VR are excluded.**
- Retains the native mounting roll, Auto Orient reference and seated FPV's Chicken Head roll correction. Camera position, focal position, FOV and vehicle controls remain native; moving targets are sampled after each native camera pose update, including rendered-frame and seated IK updates.
- **Look At Cockpit takes priority** over target locking. No target picking or lock rotation overrides that native mode.
- Manual native look input or recenter releases the lock. Switching cameras clears it. Brief target loss has a floating-origin-safe grace period before native control is restored.

## Backspace recenter

- Outside freecam, **Backspace** releases any target lock and calls the supported first-person camera's native recenter action, even without a target.
- Native roll and recenter transitions are retained. With Look At Cockpit enabled, Backspace only recenters the native observation offset; it does not override the cockpit target.
- In freecam, Backspace **still focuses on your own vehicle/avatar**. The existing freecam roll reset, dynamic-target algorithm, cinematic movement and FOV behavior are unchanged.
- The shortcut respects menus, text/chat input, the console, pause and application focus. It does not stack an active recenter animation or immediately relock from a simultaneous pick.

The first-person extension is enabled by default, independently of freecam's cinematic/general mode. Disable it in `BepInEx/config/local.sp2.freecamera.cfg` if desired:

```ini
[FirstPerson]
FocusEnabled = false
```

The selected-target shortcut shares `[Keys] FocusSelectedTarget` (default `Minus`). Backspace recenter is specific to supported native first-person views. On foot, the game's view-relative walking direction naturally follows the camera view.
